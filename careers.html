Career Development

We value people for who they are.

We can only achieve great things together if everyone is welcome. We don't just promote diversity—we see it as an essential part of delivering the best possible results to our clients, and as a result Tilleke & Gibbins is an inclusive, welcoming law firm that promotes excellence among team members from all walks of life. With more than a dozen nationalities represented in the firm, female lawyers heading up the management of all three of our regional legal departments, and a firm dedication to providing individual working solutions to meet people's unique needs—not just broad policies based on assumed traits—we are recognized both locally and internationally for our diverse and inclusive working environment. That may go some way towards explaining why we have been named an Asian Legal Business "Employer of Choice" every year since 2013.

Our managing partners are both widely-regarded as diversity champions in the industry. Darani Vachanavuttivong is consistently listed as one of the Top 250 Women in IP globally by Managing Intellectual Property, and Tiziana Sucharitkul is one of the most sought-after international speakers and commentators on diversity in the legal industry and among business executives.

We are committed to diversity, inclusion, and fairness in our hiring and in how we care for our employees. We guarantee equal opportunity for all applicants without regard to race, religion, sexual orientation, gender identity, physical attributes, or any other characteristics beyond ability to succeed.
