November 6, 2026

The 2027 edition of the IFLR1000 Asia-Pacific rankings recognizes Tilleke & Gibbins across a range of financial and corporate transactional practice areas in Cambodia, Laos, Thailand, and Vietnam.

The firm received the following rankings:

Cambodia

  • Financial & Corporate: Tier 2
  • Project Development: Tier 2

Laos

  • Financial & Corporate: Tier 1

Thailand

  • Banking & Finance: Tier 3
  • Capital Markets: Debt: Tier 3
  • Capital Markets: Equity: Tier 3
  • M&A: Tier 2
  • Project Development: Tier 2
  • Restructuring & Insolvency: Tier 3

Vietnam

  • Banking & Finance: Tier 4
  • M&A: Tier 3
  • Project Development: Tier 3

In addition to the firm rankings, 17 Tilleke & Gibbins lawyers were recognized in the 2027 individual rankings:

  • Charunun Sathitsuksomboon – M&A, Thailand; Women Leaders
  • Charupat Boon-Long – M&A, Thailand
  • David Mol – Corporate and M&A, Cambodia
  • Derrick Khoo – Financial and Corporate, Myanmar and Thailand
  • Jay Cohen – Banking, Cambodia
  • John Frangos – Restructuring and Insolvency, Thailand
  • Niti Muangkote – Banking and Finance, Financial and Corporate, M&A, and Project Development, Laos; Banking and Finance, Thailand
  • Nwe Oo – M&A and Project Development, Myanmar
  • Passanan Suwannoi – M&A, Thailand
  • Patcharaporn Pootranon – Capital Markets, Thailand
  • Phong Ngoc Nguyen – M&A, Vietnam
  • Prisna Sungwanna – Financial and Corporate, Laos
  • Saithong Rattana – M&A and Project Development, Laos
  • Santhapat Periera – Banking and Finance and M&A, Laos; Banking and Finance, Thailand
  • Saravut Krailadsiri – Thailand
  • Tram Ngoc Bich Nguyen – M&A, Vietnam
  • Yuwadee Thean-ngarm – Banking and Finance and M&A, Myanmar

IFLR1000 evaluates law firms and practitioners working in financial and corporate transactional areas, including banking and finance, capital markets, mergers and acquisitions, project development, and restructuring and insolvency. Its rankings are based on transactional evidence and client feedback.

The full results are available on the IFLR1000 pages for Cambodia, Laos, Myanmar, Thailand, and Vietnam.
