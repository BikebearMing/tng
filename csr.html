Overview

Tilleke & Gibbins is dedicated to giving back to the communities in which we live and work.

Across Southeast Asia, Tilleke & Gibbins takes its duty to corporate social responsibility seriously, focusing on the firm’s three community pillars of education, human health, and sustainability. We take part in dozens of activities every year including regular support of rural schools through equipment donations and volunteering, reforestation and wildlife preservation projects, and long partnerships with numerous organizations that seek to improve the health and quality of life of society’s least protected people.

In addition, Tilleke & Gibbins has a longstanding commitment to equality, and regularly partners with organizations that work tirelessly to support women and the LGBT+ community in business and law. While we pride ourselves on being one of Asia’s most inclusive and meritocratic workplaces, we recognize that our position comes with a duty to lead by example, and we do so at every opportunity.

Our CSR activities are not prescriptive, however, and when unforeseen events such as flooding, natural disasters, and disease outbreaks occur, Tilleke & Gibbins’ staff come together to help assist those affected in any way we can, from donations to on-the-ground support. In short, Tilleke & Gibbins is dedicated to giving back to the communities in which we live and work.
