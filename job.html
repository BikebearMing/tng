Assistant, Trademark Executive

Thailand

Department
Administrative

Ready to join us?

Reporting to the Head of Trademark Group and other designated supervisors, the Assistant, Trademark Executive supports trademark administration, client correspondence, filings, and related operational activities. The role provides an excellent opportunity to build practical experience in intellectual property practice, legal administration, and client service.

Position Summary

Trademark Administration & Case Management

  • Assist with the preparation, organization, and submission of trademark-related documents and government fees to the Vietnam IP Office and other relevant authorities.
  • Maintain accurate trademark records, filing information, mailing lists, and matter data within the Firm’s internal systems.
  • Support trademark professionals in the administration of trademark filings, renewals, recordals, and related procedures.
  • Calculate government fees and service charges for trademark and related IP matters.
  • Keep records of all administrative documentation and ensure matter information remains accurate and current.
