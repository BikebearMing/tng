Legal Professionals

Lawyers at Tilleke are the benchmark of world-class legal practice in Southeast Asia. We build sustainable relationships with our clients, achieve a deep and enduring understanding of their businesses, and work together with them to let our expertise facilitate their commercial goals in the region.

Overview

We are always on the lookout for the next legal industry leaders.

Our ever-expanding team of talented lawyers includes attorneys from across Southeast Asia and around the globe. They are multilingual and hold excellent academic credentials from the world’s most respected legal and academic institutions. In addition, lawyers at Tilleke & Gibbins have impressive experience, having worked on major cases and for leading companies in the region and beyond.

We offer meaningful professional growth and leadership opportunities to our team members to ensure that they stay at the top of the legal profession, and we challenge and support our lawyers to expand their expertise to new practice areas and jurisdictions. With highly skilled and knowledgeable lawyers in every legal field and a commitment to master emerging legal topics, our firm promotes the development of niche or broad practices that capitalize on our professionals’ skills and interests.

Career Development

Enhancing your professional and
personal development together.

We encourage all of our lawyers to develop not only as excellent practitioners but also as leaders in their field. Tilleke lawyers are respected for the breadth of their knowledge and experience, and we promote that experience by providing you with opportunities to share your insights through conferences, seminars, publications, and more. In all practices and at all levels, our practitioners hold leadership positions in chambers of commerce, charitable foundations, and bodies dedicated to furthering the legal and economic development of the region. Within the firm, we promote rising stars accordingly with pathways to leadership. By developing a consistently strong team that works and grows together, we secure a bright future for our firm to continue our legacy of excellence.
